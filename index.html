ABOUT JAI AMBE CARRIER

Moving Your Goods
With Confidence.

Jai Ambe Carrier is a transport service focused on reliable truck and parcel transportation between Surat, Vasai and Mumbai.

Our aim is to provide businesses with a dependable transportation partner for regular movement of goods.

  • Regular Surat – Vasai – Mumbai route
  • Return transportation available
  • Truck-based parcel delivery
  • Business-focused transport service

Why Choose Us?

We focus on dependable routes, straightforward communication and convenient transport solutions for our customers.


Whether you are sending commercial parcels from Surat to Maharashtra or requiring return transportation, our team is available to assist.
